# Rajasthan — RTI Request Letters (English)

**File via:** Rajasthan SSO portal ([sso.rajasthan.gov.in](https://sso.rajasthan.gov.in/) → [rti.rajasthan.gov.in](https://rti.rajasthan.gov.in/)) · Fee ₹10 · **Hindi recommended** · a Rajasthan address + ID proof is practically required for the online route.

**Key fact:** Rural reservation/rotation is determined by the **District Collector / District Election Officer (Panchayat)** (Sec. 16 RPR Act); urban ward reservation by the **State Government / DLB** (Sec. 10, Municipalities Act 2009). The SEC conducts polls and holds results. PRI general elections held: **1995, 2000, 2005, 2010, 2015, 2020.**

**Where to file:**

| Need | Address to |
|---|---|
| Rural rotation rule + framework | **PIO, Department of Panchayati Raj & Rural Development, Govt. of Rajasthan, Jaipur** |
| Rural per-district reservation rosters | **PIO, o/o the District Collector / District Election Officer (Panchayat), [District]** |
| Urban rule + ward reservation | **PIO, Directorate of Local Bodies (DLB) / Dept. of Local Self Government, G-3 Rajmahal Residential Area, C-Scheme, Jaipur-302016** |
| Elected-member results | **PIO, State Election Commission, Rajasthan, Jaipur** |

To,
The Public Information Officer (under the RTI Act, 2005),
**Department of Panchayati Raj & Rural Development, Government of Rajasthan, Jaipur.**
*(Parallel copy for district rosters: The PIO, o/o the District Collector / District Election Officer (Panchayat), [District].)*

Date: [DD Month YYYY]

**Subject:** Request under the RTI Act, 2005 — elected-member and seat-reservation records of Gram Panchayats, Panchayat Samitis and Zila Parishads, and the rule governing reservation and rotation of reserved seats.

Respected Sir/Madam,

Under **Section 6(1)** of the RTI Act, 2005, I request **certified copies of the following records**:

**A.** For the general elections to **Gram Panchayats, Panchayat Samitis and Zila Parishads** in **[District / the State]**, for each of the election years **1995, 2000, 2005, 2010, 2015 and 2020** — a copy of the existing reservation roster/record showing, for every ward/constituency: (1) name of the elected member and of the office-bearer (Sarpanch / Pradhan / Pramukh); (2) name of the Gram Panchayat / Panchayat Samiti / Zila Parishad; (3) name and serial number of the ward/constituency; (4) the reservation category (SC / ST / OBC / General, and whether reserved for Woman of that category).

**B.** A copy of the rule prescribing the **procedure for reservation and rotation of reserved seats and the offices of Sarpanch / Pradhan / Pramukh**, namely **Section 16 of the Rajasthan Panchayati Raj Act, 1994 (Act No. 13 of 1994)** read with **The Rajasthan Panchayati Raj (Election) Rules, 1994** — with all amendments and the reservation notifications/orders issued by the District Collector for the above elections.
