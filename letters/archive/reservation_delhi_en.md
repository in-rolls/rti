# Delhi (NCT) — RTI Request Letters (English)

**Key fact:** Delhi is **urban-dominant**. Rural Gram Panchayats are **defunct** (last GP elections ~1983; never revived). The substantive data is **MCD (urban)**. **MCD ward reservation applies to SC and Women ONLY — there is NO ST and NO OBC ward reservation.** MCD elections: **1997, 2002 (134 wards) · 2007, 2012, 2017 (272 wards) · 2022 (250 wards)**. NDMC is **nominated** (no elected wards). Delhi Cantonment Board is elected but a **Central/Defence** body.

**Where to file:**

| Need | Address to | Channel |
|---|---|---|
| Ward reservation/rotation rule + historical MCD election & reservation data | **PIO, State Election Commission, NCT of Delhi, Nigam Bhawan, Kashmere Gate, Delhi-110006** | [rtionline.delhi.gov.in](https://rtionline.delhi.gov.in/) or Speed Post |
| Sitting-councillor rosters / candidate-level results | **PIO, o/o Commissioner, MCD** | [mcdonline.nic.in/portal/rti](https://mcdonline.nic.in/portal/rti) |
| Historical (pre-1990) Gram Panchayat records + status of the 1954 Act | **PIO, Development Department, GNCTD** | rtionline.delhi.gov.in |
