# Karnataka — RTI Request Letters (English)

**File via:** [rtionline.karnataka.gov.in](https://rtionline.karnataka.gov.in/) · Fee ₹10 · Kannada or English accepted.

**Where to file (reservation/rotation is set by the State Govt & Deputy Commissioner — NOT the SEC):**

| Need | Address to |
|---|---|
| Rural rotation rule + framework | **PIO, Rural Development & Panchayat Raj Dept (RDPR), Bengaluru** |
| Rural per-district reservation rosters (each cycle) | **PIO, Office of the Deputy Commissioner, [District]** and **PIO, Zilla Panchayat, [District]** |
| Urban rule + ward reservation | **PIO, Directorate of Municipal Administration (DMA), Bengaluru** |
| Elected-member results (rural + urban) | **PIO, Karnataka State Election Commission, Bengaluru** |

---

**Rural Development & Panchayat Raj Department, Government of Karnataka,**
M.S. Building, Dr. B.R. Ambedkar Veedhi, Bengaluru – 560001.
*(For district-level rosters, address a parallel copy to: The PIO, Office of the Deputy Commissioner, [District], and The PIO, Zilla Panchayat, [District].)*

Date: [DD Month YYYY]

**Subject:** Request under the RTI Act, 2005 — elected-member and seat-reservation records of Grama/Taluk/Zilla Panchayats, and the rule governing reservation and rotation of reserved seats.

Respected Sir/Madam,

Under **Section 6(1)** of the RTI Act, 2005, I request **certified copies of the following records**:

**A.** For the general elections to **Grama Panchayats, Taluk Panchayats and Zilla Panchayats** in **[District / the State]**, for each of the election years **1995, 2000, 2005, 2010, 2015 and 2021** (and any other year an election was held) — a copy of the existing reservation roster/record showing, for every constituency/ward:
   1. Name of the elected member (and of the Adhyaksha/President where applicable);
   2. Name of the Grama/Taluk/Zilla Panchayat;
   3. Name and serial number of the constituency/ward;
   4. The reservation category of that constituency (SC / ST / Backward Class–A or B / General, and whether reserved for Woman of that category).

**B.** A copy of the rules/notifications prescribing the **procedure for reservation and rotation of reserved seats**, namely **The Karnataka Panchayat Raj (Reservation of Seats in Grama Panchayats by Rotation) Rules, 1998 (Notification No. RDP 28 GPS 98, dated 26.08.1998)**, **The Karnataka Panchayat Raj (Reservation of Seats in Taluk Panchayats and Zilla Panchayats by Rotation) Rules, 1998**, and **The Karnataka Panchayat Raj (Backward Classes) Rules, 1993**, made under **The Karnataka Gram Swaraj and Panchayat Raj Act, 1993** — together with all amendments and the reservation notifications issued under them for the above election years.
